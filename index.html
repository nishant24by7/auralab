Pipeline
How It Works

From image ingestion to cryptographic verdict across 4 synchronized layers.

01
Ingestion & Hashing
Accepts PNG, JPG, or WEBP. Instantly computes SHA-256 integrity hash and verifies file structure.
02
Physical Signal Scan
Runs Error Level Analysis (ELA), 2D Fourier transforms (FFT), LSB bitstream entropy, and trailing byte scan.
03
Cognitive AI Audit
Claude Vision AI reasons over physical lighting vectors, biological signatures, and threat motivations.
04
Evidence & Certificate
Generates tamper-evident forensic heatmap, 12 engine audit scores, and printable authenticity certificate.
Capabilities
12 Forensic Intelligence Engines

Parallel multi-spectrum detection suite tailored for enterprise verification and cyber forensic investigations.

--
Logical Physics Audit
Detects impossible real-world inconsistencies: shadow mismatches, reflection anomalies, and light source vector conflicts.
--
Biological Anomaly Detection
Scans for non-human facial signatures: unnatural eye-glint patterns, irregular skin pore distribution, and absent micro-vessels.
--
Thermal Heatmap Simulation
Simulates biological heat dissipation. AI faces appear thermally flat, lacking human blood-flow dynamics around eyes and nose.
--
Adversarial Noise Analysis
2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) reveals hidden grid-like noise patterns and post-processing blurring used to launder artifacts.
--
Steganography Detection
LSB pixel bitstream entropy audit and EOF trailing byte scan to detect covert malware payloads hidden inside benign images.
--
C2PA Provenance Check
Validates Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity manifests. Missing C2PA metadata on critical media is an alert.
--
Semantic Consistency Check
Verifies context coherence: weather conditions, text logic, anatomical proportions, and situational scene fidelity.
--
Social Engineering Intent
Pinpoints manipulation motivation: CEO impersonation, financial fraud, credential harvesting, or panic dissemination.
--
Batch Redline Reporting
Audits up to 20 images in parallel enterprise sessions with tabular classification and exportable triage logs.
--
Deepfake AV Alignment
Analyzes facial boundary transitions and temporal video frame warping to expose synthetic face-swap borders.
--
Fraud Risk Scoring
Unified 0-100 composite threat index weighting optical anomalies, neural scores, and provenance completeness.
--
Explainable AI Highlighting
Generates high-contrast ELA false-color visual heatmaps with interactive 10x forensic loupe for evidentiary proof.
